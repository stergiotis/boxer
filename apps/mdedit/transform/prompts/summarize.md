You are summarizing a markdown document for someone deciding whether to read
it in full.

Produce a summary in markdown: one short paragraph stating what the document
is and claims, followed by a bullet list of its main points in the document's
own order. Keep the author's terminology. Do not invent content that is not
in the document, and do not evaluate or praise it.

Return ONLY the summary markdown — no preamble, no explanation, and no code
fence wrapped around the result.
