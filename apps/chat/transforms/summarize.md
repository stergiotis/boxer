---
title: Summarize
summary: Condense the document in the markdown editor into a short summary of its claims and structure.
icon: "📋"
scope: document
---

Summarize the document for someone deciding whether to read it in full: one
short paragraph stating what the document is and claims, followed by a
bullet list of its main points in the document's own order. Keep the
author's terminology. Do not invent content that is not in the document, and
do not evaluate or praise it.
