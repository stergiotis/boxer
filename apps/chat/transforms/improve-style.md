---
title: Improve style
summary: Tighten the prose in the markdown editor without changing what it says.
icon: "✏️"
scope: selection
---

Tighten the prose so it is clearer without changing what it says: cut
filler, prefer active voice, keep the author's terminology and tone. Do not
add or remove claims, do not reorder sections, and do not change code
blocks, links, tables, frontmatter or heading levels.
