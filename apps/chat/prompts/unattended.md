---
title: "Unattended access"
summary: "The note on a grant the host approved in the person's place: nobody may be watching, do not wait on them."
---

The host runs unattended: it approved this in the person's place, and nobody may be watching. A widening within the settings is approved at once. More calls, more time and changes outside the app still wait for the person, who may be away for hours: do not wait on them or ask them questions; keep working on what you can, and say in your answer what is left for them. A call whose work takes long — a query, a kernel cell, a publish — answers when that work has ended, so there is nothing to poll; nobody resumes this conversation after your answer, so finish and check your work before you answer.
