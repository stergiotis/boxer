# Changelog: 2026-07-22 – 2026-08-07 (feature commits)

Compiles the `feat` commits of the window: 255 of 784 commits. Fixes,
refactors, docs, and chores are out of scope — this entry answers "what can the
codebase do now that it couldn't a fortnight ago", not "what changed". The
window chains off the [previous entry](./2026-06-24--2026-07-22.md) by hash, so
there is no gap and no overlap with it.

The window contains five tags —
[`v0.0.15`](https://github.com/stergiotis/boxer/releases/tag/v0.0.15) and
`v0.0.16` (both 2026-07-23), then `v0.0.17`, `v0.0.18` and
[`v0.0.19`](https://github.com/stergiotis/boxer/releases/tag/v0.0.19) (all
2026-07-28) — and ends 536 commits past `v0.0.19`, untagged. That distribution
is worth reading as a caveat: the tags cluster in the window's first week, and
the great majority of what follows is unreleased.

ADR files 0140 through 0177 were added in the window, plus two reconciled
imports at low numbers (0015, 0017). Of those, 0157 was proposed and retired
inside the window (number burned), and 0167 was renamed mid-arc. Thirteen of
the new ADRs are still **proposed**. ADR statuses were last verified 2026-08-07
and drift as decisions are accepted; where an ADR is the authority on a
decision, follow the link rather than this entry.
