# Changelog: 2026-10-11 – 2026-11-12 (feature commits)

This entry covers the feature commits of the window. Through 2026-11-06 those
are the `feat` commits: 195 of 549. From 2026-11-07 most subjects name a
subsystem and carry no type, so the 94 such commits were read one by one; the
ones that add behaviour are covered, the ones that only fix are not. Fixes,
refactors, performance work, docs and chores are otherwise out of scope. Where
one closes, reverses or replaces an arc it is cited with its type in
parentheses. Removals get the closing section. ADR statuses were read on
2026-11-12.

The first seven sections are the agent arc, in the order its pieces depend on
each other. The rest are independent and can be read in any order.

## The window in brief

This section carries no commit hashes and no links. Everything it claims is
cited in the body below.

Nothing in this window has shipped under a tag. The last tag was cut two
windows ago.

Three through-lines:

- **What the person owes an agent waits in one place.** Grant requests,
  confirmations and the model's questions are items of a host queue with
  levels and a status (Here, Busy, Away). A long task costs fewer rounds and
  approvals, and an operation may outlive the turn that started it.
- **The record is checkable outside the journal.** The chain, the vault and
  the seals moved into a record-store profile, one recorder writes every
  trail row, a trail viewer reads it, and a reply's figures can rest on
  assertions the window checks.
- **Apps took one shape.** System apps gained operations catalogs, and a
  sweep gave nearly every app a catalog, a help book and a scene that
  asserts. The chat can be specialised by a committed markdown document or
  embedded in another app's window.

Beside those: a tiled desktop, a Running tab with pause, services and
workstatelets; a block editor in mdedit; row grouping in the shared table;
entity diagrams and risk views; change points in play; a terminal widget; and
one opening through which every SQL statement leaves.

What it costs a reader: no commit carries the breaking marker, and about twenty
break something without it. The run id variable was renamed, a model call is
refused without its journal entry, and the Go side and the Rust client must be
built from one tree. Forty-five ADRs were added; fourteen are still proposed
and one is withdrawn.

## The person's attention (ADR-0323, ADR-0324, ADR-0328)

[ADR-0323](../adr/0323-attention-levels-one-table-two-scales-configurable-within-a-floor.md) and [ADR-0324](../adr/0324-the-persons-decisions-wait-in-a-host-queue-not-in-modals.md) (both accepted, `54e50f2c`)
give the host an account of what the person is shown and what they owe.

- **Levels and the queue.** An event is classified as data, message or
  highlight on the attention scale, or as your turn or critical on the waiting
  scale. Overrides move a level within its scale and never across it. The
  queue orders waiting items by level then age, keeps acknowledgements and
  shelves, and admits items per stream and per person under a flood limit.
  Host chrome draws it as a control in the menu bar and as non-modal cards
  (`ba953433`).
- **No more modals.** With the service installed, the dispatcher's grant
  requests, widenings, confirmations and moderators' questions are queue items
  decided in cards (`1f1dbef4`). The model's `ask_user` questions and exit
  approvals post there as well as in the chat window; the first surface to
  answer wins (`600be2cc`). A card answers a whole form of up to four
  questions (`550c347a`).
- **The levels as data.** An app reads its conversation's table over the bus
  and classifies locally (`7c8b8987`). The person's table is kept in the
  persistent store and edited as a grid in a Levels window (`d5ddd469`).
- **Highlights and urgency.** Highlights are the one attention level that
  reaches host chrome: counted in the control and listed apart, opening no
  card (`b86c1e13`). A conversation becomes urgent only by the person's
  confirmation in a host card, so an app cannot make itself urgent
  (`26769f9b`). F8 opens the head of the queue from any window (`58ac0b37`).
- **The person's status.** [ADR-0328](../adr/0328-the-persons-status-here-busy-away-changes-what-opens-never-what-is-decided.md) (accepted) adds Here, Busy and
  Away. The status changes what opens on its own, never what is decided. Away
  is set after an idle interval and ends at the next input, and a "While you
  were away" log lists what arrived meanwhile (`c7ac3739`).
- **Other sources.** The watchbill worker reports a failed, discarded or
  abandoned job as a notice (`be0b3539`). A window whose frame stays slow is a
  highlight (`88ae825c`, see *The desktop*).
  `keelson('attention_queue')` serves the queue as a table (`425cd003`).

## The chat window

- **A roster.** The Conversations panel groups conversations by where they
  stand: in a chat window, resumable, or closed (`d610571a`). It shows other
  chat windows' turns and raises them (`0deeda56`), a colour and a pace per
  conversation (`f5922d1c`), an "untrusted content" badge, and a group for
  runs another build wrote (`fdb57fc7`).
- **Steps by level.** Each step of a turn carries its attention level, and a
  run of finished routine steps folds under one line (`94e8a64f`). A read
  marker shows what landed while the window had no focus, and Watch raises a
  finished turn to a highlight (`b86c1e13`).
- **A dock.** The window's body is one dock: the transcript, the composer
  below it, and Conversations, Settings, Analytics and Artefact as tabs, every
  divider draggable (`b0b9197a`). The bar's buttons for those tabs are gone
  (`69d62590`).
- **Streamed answers.** [ADR-0286](../adr/0286-streamed-model-answers-over-a-private-inbox.md) (accepted in the window): the
  provider client assembles a streamed completion (`58e0bf2e`), and the llm
  service publishes coalesced deltas to an inbox only the requesting app
  instance holds (`0da4c1c4`). The chat shows the live answer paced, whole and
  as markdown, without a jump when it lands (`a28545cb`).
- **Compaction is visible.** A compaction made, refused or failed is a step of
  the turn, and the context meter reads the cut at once (`7d45eb1b`).
- **What the model is told.** The turn's context notes carry the apps the host
  has and the windows the task holds (`7461038e`). The agent surface is
  re-read on tool activity instead of on a timer (`8008b2b1`). A scripted
  model can reason before it answers, for scenes (`aa41ca96`).
- **The artefact.** Its document shows the screenshots it embeds
  (`abc306ac`). Export to lading and the handover to mdedit are under
  *mdedit*.
- **Smaller.** The conversation's trail opens in play from beside its id
  (`f9e78a59`). `keelson('agent_tools')` serves the coordinator's tools
  (`e438d06d`). A Simple view folds the bar and is remembered (`01855067`).
  Where the host registers an editor app, the composer's draft and play's SQL
  buffer can be handed to it and each save returned
  ([ADR-0365](../adr/0365-external-edit-a-text-handed-to-the-hosts-editor-app-and-each-save-returned.md), proposed; `55d83190`, `d87db262`).

## Long tasks (ADR-0355, ADR-0357, ADR-0358)

[ADR-0355](../adr/0355-what-a-long-agentic-task-costs-the-person-and-the-model.md) (accepted) fixes what a recorded long task spent on
things other than the model, each at the layer it belongs to:

- A write no longer waits on a revision that no agent-visible query reports,
  and the trail viewer's queries default to what its window shows
  (`6dcc5106`, fix). A query's arguments are optional where the window already
  holds them (`f15c51cf`).
- A bundle's own name reads its one dataset, and an unbound dataset grant
  admits it (`9eb0c3bb`).
- The window host tracks the window last given input, pins under a tiled
  layout and closes by request (`c8ad1caa`). A request opens at once where the
  person is working (`0ea1e33c`).
- More rounds go through the grant dialog, a consent is one field, and a task
  closes what it opened and keeps its coordinator in view (`4478ab13`).
- In the chat, a read of a sealed window is held once with its cost, a grant
  is asked for once, citations rest on what the model can read, and
  superseded reads leave the projection (`b1bf2466`).

[ADR-0357](../adr/0357-a-model-call-names-the-journal-entry-written-before-it.md) (accepted): a model call names the journal entry written
before it, and the service checks the entry's digest against the call
(`0fb821f9`). Write-before-act was a convention of the chat before this; it is
now enforced by the service.

[ADR-0358](../adr/0358-asynchronous-operations-a-pending-outcome-and-a-turn-a-completion-wakes.md) (accepted): a command declared `Async` stays running
until its handler says the work settled. A call still running at the end of
the wait gets a pending outcome and a watcher; when it settles, the model
reads it at the next round boundary, and with no turn running the window may
start one, marked as not from the person (`606d5f4f`). Wakes are covered by Go
tests and one scene (`b3b9f5f3`, test); the commit says that whether a model
reads a late note sensibly is for use to show.

Related: play's `query` sets the buffer, runs it and returns the first rows in
one call (`a5371ab0`). A grant request may carry the requesting app's own
sentence (`c0be1e90`). A call names the run journal its coordinator writes, so
a callee can record it (`6903bceb`). The run's tier reaches the egress wall on
agent-caused fetches, and a callee cannot lower it (`c641d026`). The
moderator's repeat signal no longer counts across a window that changed
(`c43a5cd8`). `describe` with no arguments leaves applets out again
(`024077f2`).

## Cited replies (ADR-0340)

[ADR-0340](../adr/0340-cited-replies-assertions-the-window-checks-and-verdicts-in-the-journal.md) (accepted): a claim the model draws from a result is
first stated as an assertion, a read-only statement over the tables the
result's statement read. Play's `check` runs it under the agent limits and a
scope gate, and each check is a verdict entry in the journal. The reply refers
to assertions in square brackets; the chat, the export and the artefact show
the marks, and `keelson('agent_verdicts')` serves the rows (`ba992850`). A
review tightened the scope gate and the value comparison (`118f031d`, fix).

A check may rest on any of play's last sixteen main results, not only the one
held (`b0ce84f1`), and a citation may rest on a run's answer as well as a read
(`6e73befd`).

## Evidence as a record-store profile (ADR-0326, ADR-0327, ADR-0330)

The previous window built the journal's chain, vault and commitments by hand
inside `agentrun`. This window moved them below the runtime.

- **Tuple elements with parameters.** [ADR-0326](../adr/0326-tuple-elements-carry-parameters-and-tuple-kinds-read-in-readrow.md) (accepted) lets a
  leeway tuple element carry parameters, numeric or text, and reads tuple
  kinds through `ReadRow` on sections other kinds share (`21e280fc`,
  `f412102a`). The reflection path and the read-back generator follow the
  same claim rule (`aaf98c38`, `c81cbc61`). `capmapfacts` moved from a hand
  encoder to generated components, byte-identical (`75313c52`, refactor).
- **The profile.** [ADR-0327](../adr/0327-evidence-as-a-record-store-profile-chain-vault-marks-seals-and-anchors-any-store-opts-into.md) (accepted): a store opts into chain
  and written-by components (`0ac33290`), a vault holding content behind
  per-component commitments (`4f565c8a`), a shredded component (`2442ffd0`),
  and a prepare, check, write form for a writer that must know the digest
  first (`f8f36ac6`). The laws live in one package that imports nothing from
  the runtime (`77aea4b8`).
- **The journal on it.** `agentrun` hands over what it computed by hand; a
  deletion destroys vault rows and rewrites no ledger row (`2113aadd`). Run
  and entry ids are tagged u64 values (`45999b2c`).
- **Seals and anchors.** Every durable flush of the trail writes a seal row
  chained to the last (`ffa0d537`). Anchors are keyed by scope, the seal
  stream anchors as a scope of its own, and `keelson('evidence_checks')`
  serves the laws in-process (`99f478d0`), later widened to the compaction,
  exit and copy laws (`dddd4a8a`). An anchor store can live on an
  organisation's ClickHouse server (`59f7a86f`), and an app can anchor scopes
  in a cell of its own (`312e1b5a`). The journal's laws also exist as queries,
  run in the integration lane (`368b96e8`).
- **One recorder.** [ADR-0330](../adr/0330-a-shared-audit-recorder-below-the-runtime-one-sealed-writer-over-a-narrow-store-binding.md) (accepted): the audit event and its
  stamp, invalid and gap rows moved below the runtime (`4dcec2a6`,
  `59b3386e`), a shared recorder serves a process that links no runtime
  (`3df4cd68`), and the trail became that recorder over a trail binding
  (`53301fa2`). An app writes audit events of its own over
  `runtime.audit.{app}` (`4dcec2a6`).
- **One origin.** Every writer of `boxer.facts` carries the shared Origin
  identity, so one predicate selects every kind of a run (`44dbf5bd`). The
  audit event records how a principal was established (`6f27a883`).
  State-changing events name their entity first and carry the state after
  (`390bbae2`).
- **One cell declaration.** The host hands its cell declaration to every app
  it mounts, and the run's fingerprint folds in the confined tier
  (`8abe1e73`).

[ADR-0366](../adr/0366-the-facts-envelope-says-what-a-row-is-where-it-lives-and-how-long.md) (accepted, a document only) decides a wider facts
envelope: what a row is, the bucket it belongs to and its lifecycle. Nothing
in this window implements it.

- **Views.** The model-call view gains admission, route, tier and approval,
  and the host installs the views at boot instead of waiting for a play
  window (`3f2c17a7`). `dm_journal_runs` and `dm_journal_entries` cover every
  run of the journal's ledger (`b3fa8e81`). Four `TRAIL_` SQL functions, an

- **Tables.** `keelson('inscribe_marks')` (`d6c63ab4`),
  `keelson('clipboard_copies')` (`04f6a10d`) and two more columns on
  `agent_grants` (`b18c8c21`).
- **The trail viewer.** [ADR-0350](../adr/0350-a-trail-viewer-one-system-app-over-the-marts-a-case-a-window-a-verdict-and-review-marks.md) (proposed) is a system app over
  the marts: a case and a time window, facets, the rows around a selection, a
  previous-state diff and a verdict line per writer run (`e5a4b2c8`). It
  gained exception rules shipped as data, review marks written as audit
  events, compare and as-of (`52ca1f52`); a tree tab, an actor × object
  matrix, a dotted chart, saved views and a hand-off to play (`dfae5ee9`); a
  day × hour grid and a numeric series (`caed552f`); a catalog covering every
  pane and control (`4e4d8eed`); a read lifecycle shown as one machine with
  `stop_read` (`af4bf933`); and time navigation in which every gesture lands
  in `set_range` (`cda27eab`). Replay steps the selection row by row
  (`f82d48fd`), and the detail strip shows each bucket's count (`33cb774d`).
  `mark_row` is the one system-app command with a consequential effect and is
  person-only.
- **A scorecard.** `agentscore` answers what a conversation cost and how its
  calls ended in one parameterised query over the trail and journal views

  for a benchmark take stamps its run (`6908969f`), and a refused artefact

A profile of a host found the viewer's verdict read expensive; the relation
now honours a store filter and reads each row once (`8681aea4`, perf).

  play's catalog (`f2acd72a`).

## Chatlets and the embedded chat (ADR-0364, ADR-0368)
