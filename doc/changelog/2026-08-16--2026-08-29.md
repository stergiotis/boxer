# Changelog: 2026-08-16 – 2026-08-29 (feature commits)

Compiles the `feat` commits of the window: 85 of 419 commits. Fixes,
refactors, docs and chores are out of scope — this entry answers "what can the
codebase do now that it couldn't a fortnight ago", not "what changed". Three
exceptions are made and marked where they occur: the one commit carrying the
conventional `!` marker is listed regardless of type; `fix`, `refactor`,
`build` and `docs` commits are cited where they close, reverse or supersede an
arc that would otherwise read as unfinished; and a handful of commits that
add surface without a conventional type prefix (`recordstore:`, `env:`) are
cited as if they were `feat`, since the count above does not include them.

## The window in brief

This section carries no commit hashes and no links; everything it claims is
cited in the body below.

Three tags fell in the window, and each cuts at a different arc. v0.0.20 is
the Go 1.27 move: one module bump, a mechanical `go fix` sweep, and the
retirement of the last required build tag, so a consumer needs no tags file.
v0.0.21 is the lading snapshot store: an `io/fs` tree walked into three
facts-shaped ClickHouse tables, read back as an `fs.FS` or through SQL
macros, served over SFTP for rclone, and browsed in a new app, tally. v0.0.22
is the map: Leaflet's core ported to Go on the painter lane, replacing the
walkers binding, beside a CPU-rasterized pixel host for the headless client
and a gokrazy appliance image built on that host.

Past the last tag, and unreleased: an audio waveform player with playback in
pure Go, a deterministic CBOR record form for leeway with generated per-table
wire codecs, pushout's wire moved onto the same CBOR rules, and an in-process
override tier for the env registry.

The window costs a reader one breaking rename — pushout's `graggle` becomes
`pushoutgraph`, and an existing pushout table must be dropped and recreated —
and four retirements: the egui-snarl and puffin_egui bindings, arrow-go's
parquet packages, and the walkers map. Twelve ADRs whose work had shipped
while still marked proposed were accepted in one sweep, and two ADR numbers
were reassigned after a collision, so a citation written mid-window may name
the wrong file.

## Go 1.27 (ADR-0199) — v0.0.20

[ADR-0199](../adr/0199-adopt-go-1-27.md) (accepted) was costed before it was
proposed — an adoption analysis (`29733c55`) and an M0 probe (`deb4be09`) —
and then taken as one decision (`2b68c3dd`). The `go fix` sweep ran as twenty
single-concern `refactor` commits, from dropping an obsolete `//+build` line
(`0ca8b857`) through `strings.Cut`, `slices.Sort`, `WaitGroup.Go`,
`reflect.TypeFor` and the `min`/`max` builtins to the tag-gated files `go fix`
could not see (`20f64c31`), with the two sites the `stringscut` modernizer
broke repaired (`82d8c19a`) and the cost of the sweep recorded (`4a654d89`).

The module move is one commit (`42f1dc4b`, M1) because its parts do not stand
alone: `jsontext.Token.Float` gained an error return on graduation, and
`encoding/json/v2` renamed the `,inline` struct-tag option to `,embed` and
silently ignores the old spelling — a test, not a compiler, caught that. The
`goexperiment.jsonv2` build tag then retired (`04ae73af`, M2): `./tags` goes
from two entries to one and `RequiredTags` goes empty, measured end to end
with a throwaway consumer module running boxer's CLI through `go tool` with no
tags file. The `go-json-experiment/json` module went too, since under 1.27 it
is only an alias (`e4ff19e5`, `9a8f8349`). M3 is the release: `v0.0.20` cut
and the adopter pinned to it (`d82c4697`), the bundle cut on 1.27 with TinyGo
as the cost (`031a4809`), and a note on what the milestone got wrong about how
the adopter resolves this repository (`0e72ded4`, the tag commit). The two
documents the bump made wrong were corrected (`849b9b64`), and staticcheck
runs again at v0.8.0 with one package withheld (`edc46c33`).

## The lading snapshot store (ADR-0198) and tally (ADR-0200) — v0.0.21

[ADR-0198](../adr/0198-fs-snapshot-store.md) (accepted, `c26ae2f5`) grew out
of a run of design notes on 2026-08-19 — the `io/fs` ↔ ClickHouse note
(`b5b7a7e3`), revised for stores with very many mounts (`b40824ba`), the hash
algorithm as a store parameter with BLAKE3 assessed (`9420bb27`), facts-shaped
tables with caller-owned identity (`6b786a7e`), a compact page (`8f84db27`),
and rclone as ingress and egress (`31ae331b`) — before the ADR itself with an
implementation plan (`b5d5d49f`, `a7966f04`). M0 is a trial: eleven checks
against a live server and what they answered (`1e49d485`,
[doc/trials/fs-snapshot-store-m0](../trials/fs-snapshot-store-m0/README.md)).

M1 through M6 landed on 2026-08-20: the three tables, their stores and
provisioning (`534c0f30`); walking an `fs.FS` into a snapshot (`93cc8950`);
reading a snapshot back as an `fs.FS` (`5ef8f8af`); reading it as SQL through
three macros (`870ffdbb`); an SFTP head over the store for rclone
(`049627e2`); and snapshotting a remote by borrowing rclone's backends
(`00477228`), with a how-to (`309f48b1`,
[doc/howto/lading-snapshot-store.md](../howto/lading-snapshot-store.md)). A
review pass over M0–M6 (`22be3da4`) produced the fixes that followed: four ways
a walk wrote something the read side could not use (`a9781530`), expiry,
`Sub`, block counts and `ReadAt` honoured on the read path (`61d8009a`), only
complete snapshots addressable over SFTP (`88eb9b40`), and the SQL expansion
wired without inventing a policy (`cb4e25af`). The generated stores'
`VerifySchema` was corrected twice underneath (`ec74fbed`, `769a54d9`), and
the store reserved its range in the class registry (`72a7729c`).

Beyond the milestones: slot, wildcard-mount and `'latest'` macro arguments
(`a4498cad`), with a visibility declaring what a wildcard widens to
(`9ddc8f0f`); play registers the lading mount policy kind for `LW_COMPONENT`
(`79e4bc83`); a `boxer fs snapshot` command walks a directory or an rclone
remote into the store (`70be9eb3`), and its SFTP head accepts the argv rclone
actually sends (`76c96c96`, with a how-to note on a failed handshake,
`67c79190`); a demo script seeds a store from this repository (`42c6ab6b`).

[ADR-0200](../adr/0200-tally-lading-browser.md) (accepted) is the browser,
surveyed and proposed in one commit (`61d5d358`): a file browser widget over
